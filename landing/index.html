About DOA AI Lab

This website is being used for hosting, testing and developing various AI-generated apps to support our architectural workflow directly and indirectly. Upcoming apps will ease our workflow more and make this department more efficient by providing various services to the requiring agencies and the mass people. For any inconsistency, please reach us via telephone or inbox to our official Facebook page.
